Pediatrics

Our Pediatrics service provides medical care for children as part of Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital’s healthcare services. From consultation and assessment to treatment, our medical team works to address the healthcare needs of our younger patients.

View More Details

Dialysis

Our Dialysis Center provides patients access to specialized care. Supported by trained medical personnel and dedicated facilities, the clinics are equipped to care for patients who require regular dialysis treatment.

View More Details

Smile Train

Through its partnership with Smile Train, Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital provides access to cleft care for children and adults living with cleft lip and/or palate. The hospital’s Comprehensive Cleft Care Center brings together treatment that can include cleft surgery, dental and orthodontic care, and speech therapy.

View More Details

X-Ray

Our X-Ray service helps provide the answers needed to better understand your condition. Whether it’s checking an injury, investigating symptoms, or supporting a diagnosis.
