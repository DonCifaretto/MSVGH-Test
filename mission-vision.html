Our Direction

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital in providing meaningful, accessible, and continuously improving healthcare to the community.

Our Mission

We are committed in providing the best quality healthcare services that is affordable, effective and accessible to all.

Our Vision

To be one of the leading hospitals, exceeding expectations through our continuous improvement in the quality as well as in services.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Our values shape how we care for patients, work with one another, and continue to grow as an institution.

Patients First

We strive to put our patients first, keeping their needs, dignity, safety, and well-being.

Continuous Improvement

We strive to continuously improve the quality of our healthcare practices, services, and facilities.

Teamwork

We succeed through teamwork, shared responsibility, professionalism, and mutual respect.

Care With Purpose

MSVGH was founded on the belief that healing is more than the treatment of illness. It is an act of service to humanity. Compassion, dignity, and the welfare of patients remain at the heart of the hospital's commitment to quality, affordable, effective, and accessible healthcare.

Better care begins with putting people first.
