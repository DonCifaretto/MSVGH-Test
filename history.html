Our History

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Started in 1978

Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital, Inc. began as St. Vincent General Hospital in 1978, serving the growing communities of Concepcion, Marikina, and neighboring areas. It started as a primary hospital with three wards for female, male, and pediatric patients.

As the community grew, so did the hospital and the needs of the people it served.

GROWING WITH MARIKINA

A JOURNEY OF GROWTH

The hospital reached an important stage in its development in 1993, when it became a secondary hospital. This expansion brought additional operating rooms, services, and increased bed capacity, allowing the hospital to respond to the changing healthcare needs of the community.

Established in 1978: Began as St. Vincent General Hospital, serving medical and pediatric patients.

Three Original Wards: The hospital started with dedicated wards for female, male, and pediatric patients.

Growing with Concepcion: As the population grew, the hospital expanded to meet the needs of Marikina and nearby communities.

A New Chapter in 1993: The hospital became a secondary hospital with added operating rooms, services, and bed capacity.

Expanding the Hospital: Renovations provided additional rooms as the hospital continued to grow its services and capacity.

Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital, Inc.: The organization was formally registered with the SEC on November 30, 2006, marking another chapter in its development.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Better Technologies for Better Healthcare

Today, Marikina St. Vincent General Hospital, Inc. is a Level II Tertiary Hospital housed in two buildings, with a 90-bed capacity and a workforce of more than 250 employees. Over the years, the hospital has expanded its facilities and equipment to support a wider range of medical services.

Its facilities include X-ray, CT Scan, Ultrasound, Heart Station, NICU, ICU, Emergency Room, Operating Room/Delivery Room Complex, and Dialysis Center, supported by resident doctors, specialists, and medical personnel.

With these growing facilities, the hospital continues to work toward its goal of becoming one of the leading hospitals by continuously improving the quality of its services while keeping healthcare affordable, effective, and accessible to all.
