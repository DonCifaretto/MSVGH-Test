We're Hiring: Staff Nurse

We are currently looking for a qualified Staff Nurse to join our team.

  • Bachelor's Degree in Nursing
  • Must be a Registered Nurse
  • With updated PRC license
  • Minimum 1 year of experience in a hospital setting
  • Preferably within Marikina or residing in a nearby area
  • Full-time position

Interested applicants may submit their Letter of Intent, Curriculum Vitae with photo, and other credentials via email to hr.msvgh@gmail.com or call the Human Resource Department at 8948-0314 (loc. 222) and look for MARLON MALLAPRE.
